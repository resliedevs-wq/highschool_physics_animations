3.13 Pulse-echo (ultrasound A-scan)

2d = vt λ = vf pulse length = Nλ
d
depth of the boundary or object that reflects the pulse
t
time from sending the pulse to receiving its echo; the pulse goes there and back, a distance 2d
v
speed of ultrasound in soft tissue, 1540 m s−1
f
frequency of the ultrasound
λ
wavelength in the tissue
N
number of cycles in each pulse
