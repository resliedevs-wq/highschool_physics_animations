3.11 Speed of sound with a two-beam CRO

v = fλ phase difference = 360° × Δxλ
v
speed of sound in air
f
frequency set on the signal generator
λ
wavelength: the distance the microphone moves between two positions where the Y1 and Y2 traces are in phase
Δx
distance the microphone is moved along the rule
Y1, Y2
the two inputs of the CRO: Y1 from the signal generator, Y2 from the microphone
