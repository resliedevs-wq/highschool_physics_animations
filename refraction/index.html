3.12 Refraction and total internal reflection

n1 sin θ1 = n2 sin θ2 n = cv sin C = 1n
n1, n2
refractive indices of the material the light is in and the material it goes into
θ1
angle of incidence, measured from the normal
θ2
angle of refraction, measured from the normal
c
speed of light in a vacuum, 3.00 × 108 m s−1
v
speed of light in the material
C
critical angle: the angle of incidence inside the material for which the angle of refraction into air is 90°
