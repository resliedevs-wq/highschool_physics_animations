5.9 X-ray tube

eV = hcλmin λmin = hceV P = VI
e
elementary charge, 1.60 × 10−19 C
V
accelerating p.d. between the cathode and the anode
h
Planck constant, 6.63 × 10−34 J s
c
speed of light, 3.00 × 108 m s−1
λmin
minimum wavelength of the X-rays: an electron gives all its kinetic energy to one photon
I
tube current, the rate at which charge (electrons) reaches the target
P
power delivered to the target by the electrons
