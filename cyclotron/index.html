5.2 Cyclotron

r = pBQ f = BQ2πm Ek = p22m
r
radius of the particle's path in a dee
p
momentum of the particle
B
magnetic flux density, into the page
Q
charge of the particle
m
mass of the particle
f
frequency of the alternating p.d. across the gap: one cycle for each turn of the particle
V
peak p.d. across the gap; the particle gains energy QV each time it crosses
Ek
kinetic energy of the particle
