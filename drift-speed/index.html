2.1 Drift speed

I = nAvq v = InAq
I
current; conventional current points opposite to the electrons' drift
n
number of mobile charge carriers per unit volume
A
cross-sectional area of the wire; drag the handle on the wire to change it
v
mean drift speed of the carriers
q
charge of one carrier; for an electron, e = 1.60 × 10−19 C
